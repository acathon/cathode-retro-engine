CATHODE

A retro game engine in Rust and WebAssembly. Hardware profiles are a starting position, not a cage — build against a Game Boy, an NES, a Neo Geo, or a screen you pick yourself, and choose the target when you export.

16 playable demos
628 tests
3 console presets, plus custom
0 third-party runtime deps

CARD GAMES

One deck, one bankroll, one pooled table renderer — and rules modules that never touch the engine.

Pixel Patience

examples/pixel-patience

Klondike solitaire with all 52 cards on screen as individual sprites. The rules are a pure state machine, so every legal move is unit-tested without a canvas.

464 × 416 · custom 45 tests sprite pooling

Twenty-One

examples/blackjack

Blackjack against a six-deck shoe. Soft and hard aces, the S17/H17 split, splits, doubles, insurance, and a payout table tested case by case.

464 × 360 · custom 52 tests chip betting

River Street

examples/holdem

No-limit hold'em against three bots you can tell apart. A best-five-of-seven evaluator, real side pots, and opponents whose every decision is readable.

560 × 400 · custom 83 tests side pots

Ten Card

examples/gin-rummy

Gin rummy to a hundred. What a hand is worth is a search, not a lookup — and the table draws the answer, outlining melded cards and leaving loose ones plain.

520 × 400 · custom 65 tests meld solver
